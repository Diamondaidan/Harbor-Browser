# Harbor image prompts

All images were created with the built-in image-generation tool. Beacon was selected by the user. The final icon references the concept sheet; the banner references that icon and the blue-hour artwork. Original generated PNGs were preserved; the Windows ICO is a format conversion.

## Identity choices

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Harbor Browser logo exploration comparison sheet, one landscape presentation image. Primary request: three distinct logo choices for a Windows web browser named Harbor, with practical artwork applications. Premium contemporary identity design, precise flat vector-like shapes, substantial negative space, sophisticated editorial layout on warm ivory. Three equally wide columns, large labels exactly "01 BEACON", "02 COMPASS", "03 WAVE". Beacon: geometric lighthouse using negative space and a light beam, dark navy and warm copper. Compass: simple northward compass/arrow inside a broken circular ring, petrol blue and sea-glass teal. Wave: bold single flowing wave that subtly forms an H, deep ocean blue and mint. Each column has a large isolated symbol, a clean typographic "Harbor" wordmark, a rounded-square app-icon example, tiny monochrome versions to demonstrate small-size readability, and a restrained horizontal installer/banner use sample. Add only a small top heading "HARBOR" and subheading "Identity directions". No fake UI, no photos, no ornate nautical ropes or ship wheels, no overly complex lines, no gradients in the core marks, no extraneous copy, no watermarks. This is a single comparison sheet, not a finalized identity. Make all three choices clearly distinguishable and usable.

Primary request: a beautiful contemporary coastal illustration, calm inlet bounded by layered headlands, a small architectural white lighthouse on the far right, subtle warm light in pale morning sky, still sea with delicate flowing bands of sea-glass teal and blue. Sophisticated crisp cut-paper/vector-inspired landscape with subtle fine paper texture, balanced asymmetry, warm ivory/soft sage/sea-glass teal/deep petrol palette and a tiny copper accent. Extra wide panoramic composition, upper half and center-left mostly uncluttered pale sky and mist to allow readable home-page search controls over it; landscape interest concentrated in lower third and right side. No lettering, no logo, no interface, no watermark, no ships or people. High quality standalone original background art.

## Selected Beacon icon

Use case: logo-brand. Final selected Beacon icon. Reference: the left-column Beacon app icon on logo-directions.png. Prompt: The reference image is an identity comparison board; use ONLY the LEFT column BEACON rounded-square app icon as the design reference. Produce one clean isolated square icon: dark midnight navy rounded square, bold simple white lighthouse with one small dark arched doorway, restrained warm copper light beam projecting to the right, and one clean white curved wave underneath. Centered generous padding inside tile, thick simplified shapes readable at 16-32 pixels, minimal flat color, no texture, no shading, no lettering, no decorative lines. Preserve the Beacon identity and proportions of the reference's app icon, refine for production small-size legibility. Transparent outside the rounded navy tile, real alpha. Do not include the board, other logo options, labels, text or mockups.

## Release banner

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide Harbor Browser promotional banner for release announcements and website headers. The first reference is the SELECTED Harbor Beacon icon, preserve its lighthouse/beam/wave identity. The second reference is the Harbor blue-hour landscape, use it as the visual background direction. Create an elegant panoramic horizontal banner: rich midnight navy sea and layered headlands, a tiny warmly glowing lighthouse on the right, a large refined app icon and clean white "Harbor Browser" wordmark placed together in the left third with generous breathing room. Warm copper accent underlining or tiny rule, crisp contemporary editorial typography. Keep the middle spacious. Text exactly "Harbor Browser". No slogan, no buttons, no website mockup, no version number, no Windows logo, no watermark. Should function as polished standalone release artwork, coherent with the selected Beacon identity.
